## Gameplay
When you first load into the game, your main goal is to rack up as many points as possible. You can do this by killing the enemies that are constantly spawning in the different biomes that surround you.
You have 2 weapons:
* pistol (gun): 1x multiplier
* axe: 2x multiplier

The enemies include:
* eye monster: 200 points, but 400 if you use your axe to kill it
* snowman (creeper like minecraft: 250 points, but 500 if you use your axe to kill it

Enemies keep getting stronger as your score builds up, so be careful!
You can either die by getting your health down to 0 or falling off the map somehow
